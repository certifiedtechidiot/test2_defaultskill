Frequently asked inquiries.

Contract structures, site selection, municipal permits, and structural warranties.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Custom residential estates range between 18 to 24 months depending on excavation complexity and coastal commission reviews.

Do you work on hillside topography?

Hillside engineering is our primary discipline, utilizing deep-pile caissons and grade-beam foundations for complex geology.

What contract structures do you offer?

We utilize Guaranteed Maximum Price and open-book cost-plus contracts with defined milestone disbursements.

How do you handle coastal and zoning permits?

Our in-house regulatory team manages municipal reviews, CEQA compliance, and coastal commission hearings.

Where are your architectural materials sourced?

We procure directly from verified stone quarries in Tivoli, bronze fabricators in Italy, and timber mills in the Pacific Northwest.

What structural warranty is provided?

Foundations and superstructures carry a comprehensive ten-year structural warranty alongside dedicated post-occupancy care.
