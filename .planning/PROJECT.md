# Leon Home — Luxury Construction & Real Estate Brand

## Executive Summary
Pixel-perfect replication of the luxury architectural and real estate landing page for **Leon Home**. Built to the highest standards of the unified Web Development Engine (GSD, Hallmark Anti-AI-Slop, AI Design Conversion Architecture, Taste Skill 2.5, Impeccable Craft, and OpenDesign).

## Core Offer & Audience
- **Brand**: Leon Home
- **Core Value Proposition**: "Experience Excellence In Real Estate" / "Upgrade Your Life With Smart Home Technology"
- **Audience**: High-net-worth real estate investors, luxury homebuyers, and modern architectural connoisseurs.
- **Primary CTA**: Discover More / View Properties / Contact Agent

## Technical & Design Specifications
- **Design System Tokens**: Fully declared in `:root` CSS variables (zero raw hex codes).
  - Backgrounds: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`), stark black (`#0A0A0A` / `#000000`), frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
  - Typography: Playfair Display (editorial serif) for headings and giant hero lettering; Plus Jakarta Sans (geometric sans-serif) for body, UI, stats, and badges.
  - Border Radii: `var(--radius-hero): 28px`, `var(--radius-card): 18px`, `var(--radius-pill): 9999px`.
- **Key Sections**:
  1. Hero with full-bleed luxury architectural backdrop, floating frosted glass navigation, 3D layered "LEON HOME" typography, floating stat cards ("5.9K+", "10K+"), and bottom pill CTA ("DISCOVER MORE").
  2. 3-Column Asymmetric Value Proposition with centered serif heading, brand story, partner logos, vertical 3:4 architectural photo, and stacked service features with rating badge.
  3. Team Section in stark black rounded container with 3 vertical rounded portrait cards and right-aligned editorial copy + "View All" pill CTA.
  4. Featured Projects Carousel with split heading, center-focused 3-card presentation, edge fades, glass "Available Property" badges, and interactive carousel navigation.
